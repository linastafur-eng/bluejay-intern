## Technical and founder communities

Search these alongside university/co-op, GitHub and X routes. Prioritize verified Neo Scholars and Scholar Finalists as high-potential outreach prospects; confirm the exact distinction and cohort. Verified selective technical recognition and similarly strong engineering internships are Lina's highest-priority initial-screen signals, ahead of strong attributable GitHub work and university-only signals. Record what the selection actually tested; personal work and internship interest still need review. See the [evaluation hierarchy](README.md#candidate-evaluation).

- [Neo Scholars and finalists](https://neo.substack.com/p/meet-the-2024-neo-scholars): Neo's 2024 selection process included coding tests and live technical interviews. Scholar and finalist are different distinctions; verify the person's claim and cohort.
- [Prod](https://www.prod.so/): prioritize technical alumni and former founders. Check what they built and whether they are still building the company; do not assume an old founder title means they are unavailable. The current public site does not establish a coding-screen requirement.
- [Recurse Center](https://www.recurse.com/admissions-process): admissions include a pair-programming interview. Look for early-career alumni with relevant code and debugging work.
- [Z Fellows](https://www.zfellows.com/): a pool of early technical builders working on projects and startups. Verify individual engineering contribution; fellowship membership alone does not establish a technical interview result.
- [a16z speedrun Alpha](https://speedrun.a16z.com/alpha): a software-engineer fellowship for students and recent graduates. Its Talent Track targets full-time roles, so internship interest and timing need confirmation.

For each prospect record program, exact status/cohort, membership source, selection evidence where documented, own technical work and current founder/employment status. Use public sources only. Keep internship timing and interest confirmed or unknown. This expands sourcing and outreach consideration; it does not send messages or bypass recipient-batch approval.

Program sources checked on October 12, 2026.
