# Draft recruiting emails

## Email structure

- Greeting.
- One short reason for reaching out. Prefer a relevant project when inspected; otherwise use their strongest verified presence: “Your experience at {company} caught my eye,” “Your GitHub caught my eye,” or “Your posts on X caught my eye.” Use only a source actually read; do not imply a project was inspected when it was not.
- A simple invitation: “I'm reaching out from Bluejay. We'd love to chat and hear more about what you're working on.”
- Three to five short points about Bluejay, taken from the current role brief and approved company overview. Keep them relevant to the role; do not invent traction, customers or terms.
- Link the approved Google overview document for more information.
- Sign off as the named sender.

Use the sender's writing samples and preferred phrases when available. Until then, use plain, warm language without flattery or invented familiarity. Keep the email short enough to scan.

## Learn the sender's voice

- Load the selected sender's private Notion writing guidance. Collect examples, preferred tone, length, opening, sign-off and phrases to use or avoid.
- Draft five calibration emails. Ask that sender for edits or feedback on all five; do not treat missing feedback as approval.
- Read the edits and comments with their actual author and draft reference. Separate general style preferences from person-specific corrections.
- Update the sender's guidance automatically from clear feedback. Save the previous version, new version and supporting feedback in private Notion; keep personal examples and contact data out of GitHub.
- Revise the five drafts, show the style changes, and resolve conflicting preferences. Do not change company facts, candidate criteria or send permissions through this loop.
- Use the latest guidance on future drafts. Later clear sender feedback can update it within the approved template; changed claims or structure require template review. Never apply one sender's preferences to another.

## Prepare for handoff

- Read current role facts, candidate sources, the sender's examples and [writing feedback](../../email-handoff.md). For current Bluejay facts and sender rules, use [the outreach section](../../README.md#outreach); verify facts and access before drafting.
- Save each subject/body and personalization source in private Notion. Leave missing recipient addresses Unknown; never guess.
- For Bluejay internships, the sender confirms the shared voice/template after calibration; Lina approves the recipient batch, not every email. Use [the handoff policy](../../email-handoff.md). Routine substitutions within the approved structure need no individual copy approval.
- Flag unsupported personalization, new company claims, changed sender or an out-of-scope recipient for review. Other roles retain their own approval policy.
- Direct-read saved drafts. Return the current sender-style/template versions for the Codex send workflow. Drafting and style feedback do not themselves authorize a send.

Return the drafts, updated sender guidance/version, shared template/version and any exceptions. Codex sends only under the handoff's approved batch and explicit send instruction.
