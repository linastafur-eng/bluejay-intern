## Research and review

- Explain the search direction simply. Ask only a consequential missing question; leave timing unknown unless it is an explicit requirement.
- Compare individually attributed queries across relevant work, adjacent builders and university/co-op routes. Apply Lina's initial-screen hierarchy: verified selective technical recognition or similarly strong engineering internships, strong personally attributable GitHub, then distinguished universities. University affiliation passes the initial screen but does not establish technical strength. Keep Advance/Needs verification/Deprioritize separate from the observed-work band and human decisions; popularity alone is not an ability signal.
- Search the [technical and founder communities](technical-communities.md) alongside school/co-op and open-builder routes. Prioritize verified Neo Scholars/finalists and inspect Prod alumni or former founders; verify cohort, individual work and current plans. Membership supports sourcing priority, not automatic technical scores or sending authority.
- Verify canonical identity and account links before attributing work. Inspect current work, up to three prior employers where listed, education and substantive artifacts. Fewer jobs does not penalize interns. Missing public code is Unknown. Do not execute candidate code.
- Find each candidate's public LinkedIn profile through their portfolio or a bounded name/company/school lookup. Include the verified URL beside their name in Notion, with a short identity-match note and observation date. Match explicit links or multiple consistent work/education/project details; never use a name alone. If blocked, not found or ambiguous, record that status and the next check instead of guessing. A LinkedIn profile is not evidence of email permission or availability.
- Use the README.md's intern-evidence-v1 priorities: technical depth, end-to-end product building and founder-like initiative carry similar weight; verify personal contribution and correctness across them, then consider Bluejay transfer. Return Standout / Strong / Decent / Mixed / Weak for this role / Needs verification, confidence and an evidence-based comparison. Use qualitative bands and intern-level evidence. Keep MTS scoring separate. Keep demonstrated work, fit, feasibility, interest, contactability and human decisions separate.
- Follow the checkpoints below. Count readable, attributable, role-plausible people; never pad. Sender setup does not block authorized research.
