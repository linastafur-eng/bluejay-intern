## Role perspective

The checkpoints and evaluation criteria below explain the method; private evidence and literal feedback remain in the authorized Notion workspace.

Bluejay seeks high-agency full-stack interns in San Francisco who can own features and customer problems through implementation, verification and iteration. Work spans conversational-agent evaluation, real-time infrastructure and customer deployment.

Technical depth, end-to-end product building and founder-like initiative are similarly valuable ways to show strength. A substantial project, open-source contribution, research prototype, internship or small venture can provide evidence. Assess personal contribution and correctness at intern scope, then explain how the work transfers to Bluejay.

## Review checkpoints

- **Five assessed candidates:** Lina reviews the summaries and leaves comments. Replying “done” triggers a fresh read of that feedback, revisions and continuation to ten total.
- **Ten assessed candidates:** review the additions, revisions and requested unsent drafts. Reply “reviewed—continue to 100” to authorize discovery expansion. A bare “done” triggers the feedback pass only.
- **Up to 100 distinct discovery leads:** report actual counts and remaining work. Discovery establishes role plausibility; technical assessment and recipient approval require separate review.

Codex links the exact review page and gives one next action at each checkpoint. A new chat resumes from the private run status and latest feedback.

## Candidate evaluation

Initial-screen priority:

- Verified selective technical recognition or a strong engineering internship with supported individual scope.
- Strong personally attributable GitHub work.
- Verified affiliation with a distinguished university in the preferred sourcing pools.

University-only prospects can advance while technical evidence remains Needs verification. Strong candidates outside these pools remain eligible.

Assessments use **Standout / Strong / Decent / Mixed / Weak for this role / Needs verification**, with a reason for confidence. Keep the initial screen, observed work, role fit, feasibility, interest and human decision separate. Missing public code or a short career is an evidence gap.

See the [role criteria and evidence bands](#candidate-evaluation).
